HOW TO DUEL

♜

THE GOAL

Reduce Kaiba’s Life Points from 8000 to 0, or make him run out of cards. He is trying to do the same to you.

✦

MAIN PHASE

Tap a card in your hand. Summon one monster per turn, Set monsters face-down, or Set Spells and Traps.

⚔

BATTLE PHASE

Press Battle, tap your monster, then tap an enemy monster. If the field is empty, use Direct Attack. Higher ATK wins.

◆

TRIBUTES

Level 5–6 monsters need 1 Tribute. Level 7+ monsters need 2. Tap your own monsters when asked.

⌘

TRAPS

Set Traps face-down. They trigger automatically when Kaiba attacks or Summons — no activation needed.

◉

TILT

Turn on Tilt for a parallax view. On iPhone and iPad, tap Tilt and allow motion access when iOS asks.

TIP: Tap any card to read it. Tap the same card again to close.
